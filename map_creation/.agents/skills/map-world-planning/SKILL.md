# Allocate a world

Read [world planning](../../../docs/world-planning.md) and [world format](../../../docs/world-format.md). Use the user's setting as canon, and mark new proposals. Never publish hidden GM explanations in public records.

Establish coastlines, high ground, valleys, complete drainage systems and habitats before local ornament. Trace each river to a receiving sea, lake or deliberately closed basin. This illustrative plan is not a physical hydrology model. Distinguish a graph check from visible bank continuity.

Separate countries, provinces, settlements, mobile communities, nonterritorial networks and threat areas. Give sovereign entities room for centers, supporting land and journeys. Preserve meaningful secure cores as well as wilderness and contested margins. Do not force each faction into an exclusive country polygon.

Use [societies.md](../../../templates/societies.md) for public population and provision assumptions. At local scale, record residents, household occupancy, shared facilities and visitors separately. At atlas scale, treat buildings as pictorial markers, not census data. Food, water, fuel, storage and trade need explicit assumptions; fields alone cannot establish annual capacity.

Give major routes users, destinations and crossings. Test crowded areas at the intended marker footprint and whole-map viewing size. If the world is compressed, change geographic allocation rather than just increasing resolution.

Save a valid `world.json`, a society ledger, key journeys and uncertainties. Render a schematic guide using `mapkit.py guide`; review its planning relationships before painting. The helper validates IDs, bounds and basic river graph connections, but not land membership, terrain elevations, political polygon overlap or food supply. Perform those reviews explicitly.

Account for every required site in each region brief. After painting, record observed anchors separately; move canon or planned positions only with a reason and appropriate existing authorization.
