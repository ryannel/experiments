# Review what the pixels show

Use an overview for hierarchy, regional crops for relationships, and native crops for structure. Technical validation and visual review answer different questions. Record `pass`, `fail` or `unclear`; a required unclear check is not acceptance.

## Geography and movement

Trace each river from its source through confluences, bridges, woodland and joins to its actual receiving water. Trace both banks back upstream. Inspect the last mouth pixels: a beach, surf line, quay or blend mask can close an apparently open mouth. A tile-edge exit remains a pending continuation. An open harbor entrance does not establish an unobstructed upstream channel.

Follow each road continuously through gates and junctions to its destinations. A line visible on both sides of a wall is not a passage. Check useful width for people, carts or animals; a pen or cottage can pinch a previously accepted route. Check inherited geometry too.

## Form and integration

Review camera, lighting, terrain material, canopy variety, building silhouette, ground contact and repetition. Check all mask edges for clipped roofs, double banks, detached gates, fence remnants, blurred architecture and floating shadows. Count literal buildings only at a representation scale where the count means something.

## World intent

Check every required site intersecting the candidate, including sites omitted by a narrow local brief. Record each as painted, moved with destination, deferred with reason, or unresolved. Look for distinct territorial character and genuine quiet ground. An unusual landmark should read as intentional, not as malformed structure.

## Record evidence

Fill the candidate's generated review file after inspecting its images. Each required check needs observations and existing evidence paths. Include full-route evidence when a river or journey leaves the candidate; local crops cannot establish whole-catchment validity. The helper checks that records and hashes exist, but cannot verify that the reviewer truly looked or that their judgment is correct.

User approval is a separate field. Record it only when actually supplied. Revise locally after failures, using a new candidate ID. Recheck surrounding systems after the fix rather than approving only the repaired close-up.
