# Geography, people and political space

## Geography before decoration

Choose broad land masses, mountain divides, lowlands and receiving seas before drawing local streams. Record complete catchments in world coordinates. A normal tributary joins a downstream channel; a lake usually has one surface outlet. Closed basins, tidal distributaries and channels around islands are deliberate exceptions that need visible terrain support. A decorative fork introduced during painting is a defect, not new canon.

Plan biomes through latitude, height, shelter, exposure and moisture as illustrative assumptions. This kit does not run a climate or elevation model. Keep agricultural lowlands, wet floodplains, exposed ridges and forest transitions legible, and reserve quiet wilderness.

## Use separate political concepts

A country, province, settlement, trade network and threat region are different things. A league may contain independent states, each needing settlements and intervening territory. A guild can span countries without owning a unique land polygon. Represent overlapping reach explicitly in the society ledger.

Allocate secure cores, patrolled roads, contested margins and unpoliced interiors. A formidable realm should have believable safe country. Give hostile communities resources and access where they exist, but do not insert an enemy army beside every allied capital to manufacture adventure.

## Population assumptions

Choose an illustrative population range or a specific model, and say which. At local scale, a simple ledger can be `households × average occupancy + communal residents`, with visitors counted separately. A barn is not another household. At world scale, pictorial roofs are symbols, not a census.

Record food catchment or imports, water, fuel, storage, work and trade for each settlement role. Put specialized services where the surrounding demand could support them; one provider can serve several communities. Distinguish households from jobs and seasonal visitors. If land area, yields or water recharge are not modeled, do not infer annual sufficiency from attractive fields or a spring icon.

The [society template](../templates/societies.md) contains public planning tables. Hidden causes, covert sites and unrevealed factions belong outside public world JSON.

## Travel before roof placement

For each important journey, record endpoints, why someone travels, travel mode, obstacles, crossings, and an estimated distance/time only when a distance scale exists. Gate and doorway access matter as much as road centerlines. On an atlas, exaggerated markers should not imply that three countries fit into one village district.

Validate political room using marker footprints and intervening landscape. Adding pixels cannot fix a fundamentally crowded allocation. Move anchors with an explicit reason, and reconcile labels with actual painted sites after generation.
