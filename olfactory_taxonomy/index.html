Read the story on ryannel.dev ↗

The wheel is a selective guide, not an exhaustive classification. Material references illustrate scent facets; they do not identify ingredients in the perfume you are smelling.

The making

Photographs, structure, language.

This repository contains the finished product and the ingredients for making it: the taxonomy, all 130 image prompts, generation briefs, reusable skill, editable SVG master and exploration code. The questions are built from the same local data as the wheel.
