# Accepted lettering

Use the existing editable SVG lettering as the source of truth. Baskerville headings and Avenir body labels use warm ivory fill with a fine dark edge, tight glyph-following dark fade and wider translucent dark fade. No rectangular or oval label panels and no white glow.

The spacious edition's native hierarchy is 96-pixel realm headings, 72-pixel families (61.5 for Musk; 57 for Ethereal and sweet Tobacco), 48-pixel characters, 42-pixel comparisons, 36-pixel feel words and 33-pixel references. Preserve curated wraps and reviewed image-aware offsets. Readable supporting text matters more than exaggerated size hierarchy.

Realm names follow editable text paths. Long names split into “EARTHY, WOODY” / “& DEEP” and “AMBER, SPICE” / “& SWEET”; the other two use one line. Blocks centre on optical radius 520, with 78-unit radial line spacing. Reverse lower arcs to keep reading upright. All four realms use a 0.45-pixel hard edge, 0.95-pixel close dilation and 0.86 close-shadow opacity; retain their diffuse shadow. Smaller outer lettering keeps the stronger shared contrast recipe.

The central disc radius is 220 units. OLFACTORY and ATLAS are 42 and 78 SVG units (63/117 physical pixels), with baseline offsets -28/+50, tracking 2/3 and a rule inset of 12. Values are recorded in data/spacious-typography.json; source/master.svg holds actual placement and filter definitions.

Check actual browser rendering, especially pale foliage, soft/dry labels, small reference type and narrow families. Do not infer legibility from mathematical fit or a fitted overview. Fonts are referenced, not distributed; changed fonts require a new fit check. Rebuild labelled tiles after edits and both tile modes after geometry or photography changes.
